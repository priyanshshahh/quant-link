# QuantLink — Demo Video Script (~2:30)

**Live:** https://quantlink.vercel.app · **Repo:** https://github.com/priyanshshahh/quant-link

## How to record (macOS, 30 seconds of setup)
1. Open the game in Chrome **fullscreen** (Cmd+Ctrl+F). Hard-refresh first (Cmd+Shift+R) so the latest build loads.
2. Press **Cmd+Shift+5** → choose **Record Entire Screen** (or "Record Selected Portion" around the browser) → **Options → Microphone: MacBook Mic** (for voiceover) → **Record**.
3. Stop with the menu-bar ■ icon or Cmd+Ctrl+Esc. Trim in QuickTime if needed.
> Tip: Open a 2nd browser tab/window logged in as a different name to show **multiplayer** (two traders in one world).

## Shot list + voiceover

### 0:00–0:15 — Hook
- **Show:** the city skyline at spawn, neon glowing, character idle, slowly pan the mouse.
- **Say:** "This is QuantLink — a GTA-style 3D quant-trading life-sim where the entire market is a live, multiplayer simulation running on SpacetimeDB."

### 0:15–0:35 — World + movement
- **Do:** Walk with **WASD**, **Shift** to sprint, mouse-look around. Drive past the cars, palms, and the live ticker board.
- **Say:** "Every price you see on the ticker is server-authoritative and streaming to all players in real time. I'm running a hedge fund in this city."

### 0:35–1:05 — Trading
- **Do:** Walk to the **QuantLink Exchange** (north), press **T**. Buy a couple positions (e.g., NVDA, BTC). Show the price flashing and your portfolio/net worth update.
- **Say:** "I open the terminal, buy some positions — the trade hits a reducer, the server updates my portfolio, and my net worth recalculates live."

### 1:05–1:30 — AI Market Wire + Remix
- **Do:** Point at the **top news ticker** when an AI headline drops. Then in the terminal's Market tab, type a **remix prompt** like *"AI mania, moon NVDA, crash oil"* and submit. Show prices react.
- **Say:** "An AI market wire invents breaking news every minute that violently moves prices — and I can literally type a prompt to remix the market for everyone in the world."

### 1:30–1:55 — Firm, lifestyle, objectives
- **Do:** In the terminal, hire an **employee**, buy a **vehicle** or **property**. Then press **Q** to open **Career Objectives** and **claim** a completed one (watch cash jump).
- **Say:** "I build my firm — hire a desk, buy real estate, upgrade tiers — and complete server-validated career objectives for cash bonuses."

### 1:55–2:15 — Leaderboard + multiplayer
- **Do:** Show the **Rich List** overlay (top-right). If you have a 2nd window open, show both players in the world.
- **Say:** "A server-computed Rich List ranks every player by net worth in real time — it's fully multiplayer."

### 2:15–2:30 — Close
- **Show:** pull the camera back over the glowing city.
- **Say:** "QuantLink — real-time multiplayer, AI-driven markets, an open 3D world. Built solo on SpacetimeDB. Thanks for watching."
